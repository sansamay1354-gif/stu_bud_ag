Project Summary
The StudyBuddy Agent is an AI-driven platform designed to enhance university students’ learning experiences by facilitating peer connections, smart scheduling, and interactive study sessions. It aims to improve academic success through effective collaboration and resource sharing among students.

Project Module Description
Dashboard: Displays upcoming study sessions and matched peers.
Peer Finder: Allows users to search and filter peers by subjects, availability, and location.
Sessions Management: Enables the creation and management of study sessions with calendar integration.
Learning Resources: Provides access to study materials and tools.
Profile Management: Allows users to manage their profiles, academic interests, and preferences.

Usage
